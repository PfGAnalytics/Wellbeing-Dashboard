PfG Wellbeing Dashboard - Quick read

All Domains Performance

Across all domains, there are currently:

Thriving Children

Our children and young people have the best start in life

The Thriving Children domain accounts for:

  • Children's social care
  • Life satisfaction among young people
  • Self-efficacy among young people
  • Small for gestational age

Cleaner Environment

We live and work sustainably - protecting, improving and enjoying the environment

The Cleaner Environment domain accounts for:

  • Air pollution
  • Coastal water condition
  • Condition of protected sites
  • Greenhouse gas emissions
  • Outdoor recreation
  • Recycled waste
  • Renewable electricity
  • River water quality
  • Sustainable travel - public transport

Equal Society

We have an equal and inclusive society where everyone is valued and treated with respect

The Equal Society domain accounts for:

  • Cultural identity
  • Income deprivation
  • Income inequality
  • Respect

Healthier Lives

We all enjoy long, healthy active lives

The Healthier Lives domain accounts for

  • Active travel - walking and cycling
  • Healthy life expectancy (females)
  • Healthy life expectancy (males)
  • Mental health
  • Preventable deaths
  • Sport and physical activity

Brighter Futures

Everyone can reach their potential

The Brigther Futures domain accounts for:

  • Arts and cultural activities
  • Life satisfaction
  • School leavers attainment gap
  • Self-efficacy

Stronger Economy

Our economy is globally competitive, regionally balanced and carbon-neutral

The Stronger Economy domain accounts for:

  • Economic inactivity
  • Employment rate
  • External sales rate
  • Good jobs
  • International reputation
  • Labour productivity
  • Northern Ireland Composite Economic Index
  • Research and development
  • Skills

Safer Communities

Everyone feels safe, we all protect the law and each other

The Safter Communities domain accounts for:

  • Crime prevalence rate
  • Personal safety
  • Processing times for criminal cases
  • Safe towns and city centres
  • Worry about crime

Caring Society

We have a caring society that supports people throughout their lives

The Caring Society domain accounts for:

  • Adult social care
  • Loneliness
  • Volunteering

Better Homes

Everyone has access to good quality, affordable and sustainable homes that are appropriate for their needs

The Better Homes domain accounts for:

  • Availability of suitable housing
  • Homelessness
  • Housing costs as a proportion of household income
  • Housing stress

Living Peacefully

We have a lasting peace, ensuring a stable and harmonious society for all

The Living Peacefully domain accounts for:

  • Community relations
  • Shared community
  • Trust in the media
  • Trust in the NI Assembly
