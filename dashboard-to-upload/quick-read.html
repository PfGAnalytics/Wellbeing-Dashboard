Society wellbeing quick read

Why do we have a wellbeing framework?

The wellbeing framework is like a health check. But instead of being for one person, it is for us as a society.

To create it, we look at social, environmental and economic facts and figures. Because these are the things that allow our society to flourish.

Ten key factors for wellbeing

To create the wellbeing framework, the NI Executive picked 10 areas to look at. They call these “domains”. Improving these 10 domains helps us all to lead healthy, safe and fulfilling lives.

The 10 domains include things like:

  • Health
  • Education
  • Jobs
  • Safety
  • The environment
  • Strong communities

What can the 10 domains tell us?

Each domain of wellbeing has a few indicators that we can measure. By looking at these indicators and domains, we can see:

  • how Northern Ireland is doing across different areas of life
  • track progress over time, so we can see if things are getting better
  • highlight areas that need more work to improve people’s lives
  • provide evidence to support decision-making in government

This document tells you how well we are doing for each of those areas. It is based on information available to government.
